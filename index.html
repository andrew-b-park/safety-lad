Safety-LAD (Ladder Audit and Diffusion Data Set) is a data set of construction ladder scenes judged against five ladder rules from OSHA 29 CFR 1926.1053 and ANSI A14.5-2017: 20,000 generated frames and 1,230 real photos from 82 videos. Each generated image is photorealized (repainted by an image model) from an Isaac Sim 3D render; the render, not the photorealized image, is pixel-aligned with depth, normals, segmentation and keypoints. Verdicts come from measured 3D geometry; ground is the scene’s set condition.

Real photos

1,230 photos from 82 videos · 4 files each, plus one record per video

Three real ladders (a step ladder, a platform ladder and an extension ladder) filmed with one phone in a gymnasium and at an outdoor loading dock, with and without a climber. Each photo has ladder keypoints with a triangulated 3D angle, the rule verdicts (the same rules, applied to the setup measured on site), a hand-drawn ladder mask and worker mask, and the camera pose. Faces are blurred.

At real/<folder>/R01_001358.<ext>, all in safety-lad_real.tar. Split by video: train 21 videos (315 photos), val 11 (165), test 50 (750).

Verdicts
compliant, violation or N/A (reason); the roll-ups overall_verdict, geometry_assessment and ground_assessment are never N/A.
Rule fields
angle_rule on angle_deg: 29 CFR 1926.1053(b)(5)(i). osha_3ft_rule on osha_3ft_extension_m: 1926.1053(b)(1). rail_above_feet_rule on rail_above_feet_m: ANSI A14.5-2017. top_step_rule on steps_from_top: 1926.1053(b)(13). ground_assessment on ground_condition and ground_contaminant: 1926.1053(b)(6), set for the scene, not measured.
Violation types
wrong_angle, insufficient_extension, above_highest_standing_level, top_step_use, slippery_surface, contaminated_surface
Units
Metres (_m), degrees (_deg) and pixels throughout; values that do not apply are null. scene_context is the intended scene (provenance: generation_intent), not an observation.
Coordinates, keypoints and classespixel and 3D frames, keypoint order, COCO and YOLO categories, semantic key
Pixels
Origin at the top-left corner of the picture, x to the right, y down; the centre of the pixel in column c, row r is (c + 0.5, r + 0.5). Label boxes are x_min, y_min, x_max, y_max; COCO boxes [x, y, width, height]; YOLO centre x, centre y, width, height over the picture size.
World
Right-handed, z up, metres: the scene’s frame for generated frames, the ChArUco marker board’s for real ones. keypoints_3d, obb_3d and camera.position are in it.
Camera
camera.intrinsics holds fx, fy, cx, cy in pixels; camera.distortion is zero (renders are pinhole, real photos are undistorted). world_to_camera and camera_to_world are 4 × 4 on column vectors with camera axes x right, y up, z backward; OpenCV axes are diag(1, -1, -1) times them. With p = diag(1, -1, -1) (world_to_camera [X, 1])[:3]: u = fx px / pz + cx, v = fy py / pz + cy, which reproduces keypoints_2d.

The COCO order. Labels name the same points (and *_mid points) in keypoints_2d (pixels) and keypoints_3d (world, metres). A slot that is not a separate point on that ladder (null, or within 1 cm of an earlier slot, as contact = top on an A-frame) has visibility 0. The viewer draws the first eight.
Worker keypoints
23 (DWPose-23): COCO-17 (nose to right_ankle), then left_big_toe, left_small_toe, left_heel, right_big_toe, right_small_toe, right_heel. Generated only; real records have none.
Visibility
2 in the picture and not occluded, 1 in the picture and occluded, 0 not labelled. A point behind the camera or outside the picture is written (0, 0, 0) in COCO and YOLO; labels keep its raw projection.
Categories
COCO: 1 ladder, 2 worker; background workers, the marker board and passers-by are iscrowd: 1 ignore regions. YOLO: 0 ladder, 1 worker, ignore regions left out. COCO image ids equal labels frame_id.
Semantic
semantic_fine (26 classes) and ladder_parts are tabled in semantic_classes.json; instance ids are listed per frame in labels instance_ids.

Files on the UltraViolet record

Every bundle unpacks into the same folder tree, so any set of bundles can be combined. The core tars hold the photorealized image, Isaac render, labels and masks; the geometry tars add depth and normals. Train, val and test are a column in frame_index.json, not separate files. All 63 files are on the UltraViolet record.

Which files do I need?
2D detection or keypointsthe core tars of the cells you need, annotations.tar, code.tar
Segmentation, verdicts, 3Dthe core tars (labels and masks are inside)
Depth or surface normalsadd the geometry tars of the same cells
Real benchmark onlysafety-lad_real.tar, annotations.tar, code.tar
The paper’s detectorssafety-lad_weights.tar
Choose frames firstframe_index.json: filter, then fetch only the tars it names
After downloading
  1. Check: sha256sum -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS (macOS: shasum -a 256 -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS)
  2. Unpack in one folder: tar -xf name.tar; geometry: tar --zstd -xf name.tar.zst
  3. Check every file inside the tars: python safety-lad/code/verify.py .
  4. Draw one frame’s labels: python safety-lad/code/load_example.py . --id gen_00042
